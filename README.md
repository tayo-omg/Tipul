# Tipul

Tipul is an AI-powered hospital follow-up platform designed to make follow-up care faster and easier for patients and healthcare professionals.

## The Problem

Patients often spend hours at hospitals for follow-up consultations that may only require a few minutes with a doctor.

## Our Solution

Tipul allows existing hospital patients to communicate with their doctors remotely after their initial physical consultation.

Patients can:
- Send follow-up updates about symptoms and recovery
- Discuss how prescribed medication is working
- Receive information about prescriptions
- View test requests and results
- Schedule follow-up appointments
- Receive referral information

## How AI Is Used

Tipul's AI does not diagnose or prescribe medication.

The AI:
- Summarises the patient's latest message
- Retrieves relevant information from the patient's previous hospital records
- Highlights important changes between previous and current information
- Presents a concise follow-up summary to the doctor

The doctor reviews this information and makes the final clinical decision.

## Example Workflow

Patient visits hospital → Doctor examines patient → Patient uses Tipul for follow-up → AI combines new information with relevant patient history → Doctor reviews → Prescription, test, appointment or referral.

## Main Features

- Patient follow-up portal
- Doctor dashboard
- AI patient-history summarisation
- Appointment scheduling
- Pharmacy integration
- Test and result management
- Hospital referral coordination

## Prototype

This repository contains the front-end prototype of Tipul developed for a healthcare innovation hackathon.

## Important

Tipul is designed to support healthcare professionals, not replace them. Diagnosis, prescriptions and other clinical decisions remain the responsibility of qualified healthcare professionals.
